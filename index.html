05Hill Climbing · door search

RESCUE COMPLETE

Ashley is safe. Search five enchanted doors using only immediate left and right neighbors. Danger levels are randomly generated from 1 to 99; lower is safer. The search can get stuck at a local minimum.

How this Hill Climbing search works: each door is a state and safety score = −danger. Only the immediately adjacent doors are neighbors (one at either end, two in the middle). At each step, Run compares those neighbors and moves to the lowest-danger neighbor only if it is strictly safer. It repeats from that door, stopping when no adjacent door improves safety. Equal danger is not an improvement. This local minimum may not be the safest door overall.

Click a door to choose a starting state. Random Restart keeps the same dangers, chooses a different random start, and runs again. Reset Simulation or load a map to close this panel; the next rescue generates new danger levels.

02Enemy settings

LIVE PARAMETERS

Change the odds, change the route. Settings apply to every active enemy of that type and immediately replan A*.

What do these parameters mean?
  • Win probability: chance Leon defeats the enemy.
  • Victory reward: positive points earned on a win.
  • Loss penalty: positive magnitude subtracted on a loss.
  • Combat effort: points spent whenever a fight is attempted.

Realized net reward = victory rewards − loss penalties − effort. Walking and A* route costs are planning units, tracked separately from combat points. Inputs accept probabilities 0–100% and nonnegative values up to 1,000.

Local choice ≠ global route. The MDP compares immediate Fight vs. Avoid (0). A* compares complete routes. Even a locally unattractive fight can beat a long detour. Entering an enemy tile always requires combat.

MDP states, transitions & rewards

States: Enemy Active, Enemy Defeated, Leon Defeated. Actions at Enemy Active: Fight or Avoid. Fight outcomes end this local encounter; Leon Defeated also ends the mission.

Avoid keeps the enemy active with probability 1, reward 0, and requires a different route. This is a one-step horizon: no future state value is added. The defeated states have no further actions in this encounter.

Why the A* heuristic is admissible

EnemyTileCost = 1 + max(0, −Q(Fight)) is an explicit game-design conversion from reward to cost, not an MDP equation. All moves cost at least 1, so Manhattan distance never overestimates remaining cost. A* tracks its open set, g scores, f = g + h scores, and parents to reconstruct the cheapest route. Positive rewards never create negative costs or repeatable walking bonuses.
